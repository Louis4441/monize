{{- if eq .Values.cluster.mode "multi" }}

CLUSTER_MODE=multi: two things this chart cannot check for you.

  1. backend.database.DATABASE_HOST must reach a PostgreSQL *session*.
     Every replica holds one LISTEN open for cross-replica wake-ups, and a
     transaction-mode pooler (pgBouncer in its default mode) drops it: the
     connection succeeds and the notifications never arrive. A direct service
     -- CNPG's "-rw", a plain postgres Service, a session-mode pooler -- is
     what this needs. The backend refuses to boot if it cannot hold the LISTEN,
     so a misconfiguration is a log line rather than a slow application.

  2. The storage assertions must be true, not merely set.

{{- if not .Values.backend.persistence.backups.enabled }}

NOTE: automatic backups have nowhere durable to write.

  The backend runs with readOnlyRootFilesystem: true and no volume is mounted at
  {{ .Values.backend.backupContainerDir }}, so a user who turns on a backup
  schedule in Settings will get a schedule that reports errors and produces no
  files. The UI does not know that, so it will look configured.

  To fix:  --set backend.persistence.backups.enabled=true
  Or mount your own claim with backend.persistence.backups.existingClaim.
{{- end }}
